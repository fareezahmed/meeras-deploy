Islamic Will Generator for Australia

A free, private web application that helps Muslims in Australia prepare a Last Will and Testament that is Shariah-compliant and designed to meet the formal requirements for a valid will in every Australian State and Territory.

Community-maintained, free and open-source. Your answers never leave your device; we collect only anonymous, cookie-free page-view statistics (Umami on our own server, and Metricool).

Inheritance shares in Meeras follow the Al-Mawārīth engine. Work out your family's shares, with the evidence for every rule, and have them reviewed by a scholar.
