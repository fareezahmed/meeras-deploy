# Al-Mawārīth + Meeras: promotion material for Australia

One campaign for both sites, built on a two-step message: **work out the shares
(almawarith.com), then put them in a Will (meeras.com.au)**. Made for a small first
round: WhatsApp groups, community Facebook groups, Instagram and mosque notice boards.

Nothing in this folder is deployed to either website.

## 2. Wording rules

These come from the launch plan ("Rules for every campaign") and apply to anything
written around these images.

- Lead with the problem (dying without a Will) and the price (free).
- Say **"designed to meet the formal requirements"** and **"under independent legal
  review"**. Do not say "legally valid", "lawyer approved" or "guaranteed".
- Call both tools **guides**. Neither gives legal advice or a binding ruling.
- Do not answer questions about someone's own family or Will in comments or messages.
  Point them to a scholar and an Australian lawyer.
- Privacy: Meeras answers stay on the person's device. Do not promise the same for
  Al-Mawārīth's scholar review, where the person sends their details by choice.

## 8. A small first round

1. Post `social-1` with the WhatsApp message in two or three groups you belong to.
2. A few days later, post `social-2` or `social-3` in the same groups.
3. Print five to ten flyers and ask one or two masjids to display them.
4. After two weeks, check each site's statistics for visits tagged `au-launch` before
   widening the circle.

The launch plan still lists three items as open before wide public promotion: the
solicitor's written view on legal practice, insurance, and registering "Meeras" as a
business name. Keep this round small until those are done.
