For Muslim families in Australia

Know your shares.
Write your Will.

If you die without a Will in Australia, your State’s intestacy rules decide who inherits. Those rules do not follow the Qurʾānic shares, and they say nothing about an Islamic burial or who raises your children. Two free tools, made for our community, help you put it right.

Step 1 · Calculate

Al-Mawārīth
inheritance calculator

  • The Qurʾānic share of every heir, from spouse and parents to distant relatives
  • Explains why each person inherits or is excluded, with the evidence
  • Choose your school: Ḥanafī, Mālikī, Shāfiʿī or Ḥanbalī
  • Download a report for your family and executor
Scan, or visitalmawarith.com
Step 2 · Write

Meeras
Islamic Will

  • Shares, Islamic burial wishes, guardians, executors, debts and bequests in one document
  • Designed to meet the formal requirements of every State and Territory
  • Made on your own device; your answers are not uploaded
  • About twenty minutes, then print and sign with two witnesses
Scan, or visitmeeras.com.au

Three steps, one evening

  1. Calculate. List who would survive you and see each share.
  2. Write. Answer the questions and download your Will.
  3. Sign. Print it, sign before two adult witnesses, and tell your executor where it is.

Al-Mawārīth and Meeras are guides. They are not legal advice and not a binding Sharīʿah ruling. The Meeras Will template is under independent legal review. Have the shares confirmed by a qualified scholar, and see an Australian lawyer if you own a business or property overseas, have a blended family, or want to leave someone out.
